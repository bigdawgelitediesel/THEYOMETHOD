# The Yo Method - Content Plan

Jacob's side of the system. Yolanda sees the filming list at the live site; this is the thinking behind it and the posting side.

## Goal

Build Yolanda's personal brand on Instagram so that The Yo Method (Fort Collins classes, online, privates) fills up from people who feel like they know her.

Success looks like: local women in Fort Collins / NoCo DMing to book, online clients from outside Colorado, and a following that is big enough to matter when the TV project goes public.

## Content pillars (rotate through these every week)

| Pillar | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Her** | Talking to camera, day in the life, mom of 3 moments | People follow people. This is what makes them care. |
| **Teach** | 3-move tutorials, form tips, "do this if..." | Gets saved and shared. This is how strangers find her. |
| **Show** | Class footage, movement reels to music | Proof she is good at this. Sells the classes. |
| **Grid** | Photo carousels, portraits | Makes the profile look like a real brand when someone lands on it. |
| **Tease** | Hints about what's coming (the show, new offerings) | Keeps people watching. Use sparingly. |

Default weekly mix: 2 Her, 2 Teach, 1 Grid (Show when there is real class footage). Swap a Her for a Tease when there is something to tease.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Jacob | Add next week's posts with "+ New post" on the Jacob side. Text Yolanda "list is up." |
| Mon-Fri | Yolanda | Films per the calendar (Wednesdays off). Checks off on the site. Uploads to Drive. Taps Sent. |
| As they land | Jacob | Edit, caption, bank them. Post every 3 days from the bank. |
| Daily | Jacob | Post stories from whatever raw clips she sent. Reply to comments / DMs within the day. |

Posting cadence: one post every 3 days once the bank is built. Posting time to start: 7:30 pm Mountain. Adjust after 3 weeks of data.

## Rules

- Never post about church or Ryan. If a commenter brings it up, don't engage, don't delete unless abusive.

- Students / clients in footage: she gets permission or shoots from behind.
- The boys: faces are her call, every time. Default to backs of heads.
- No AI-looking captions. Short sentences. Her words from the memo. No em-dashes.
