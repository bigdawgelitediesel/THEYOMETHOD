About Yolanda

I found Pilates the way most moms do.

Tired, sore, and looking for something that fit between school drop-off and dinner.

I spent years at home raising three boys before I found Pilates. Nobody told me three kids would make me feel like a stranger in my own body. Pilates changed how my body handled the long days, and it worked well enough that I got certified and started teaching.

The Yo Method is my way of doing it. Group classes here in Fort Collins. Live online sessions for people who can't make it in. And private one-on-one work for anyone coming back from a baby, an injury, or a long time away from their own body.

When I'm not teaching I'm on a ballfield, in a pickup line, or on my own mat.
